About Dominican Republic Vacation Spots

Updated November 10, 2026

The Dominican Republic is more than one resort coast. This bilingual guide compares the main vacation spots, from Punta Cana and La Romana in the east to Puerto Plata, Cabarete and Samaná in the north and the Colonial Zone in Santo Domingo, so you can choose by the kind of trip you want.

Who publishes it

Published by Eye To Ad Media, an independent web publisher in Denver, Colorado, run by Zach Wennstedt. We're a guide, not a travel agency.

How we keep it useful

  • Entry rules, fees and safety advice can change; we link to official sources so you can check the current version.
  • No fabricated reviews, prices or ratings.
  • English and Spanish versions say the same thing.
